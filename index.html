Our Mission

Finding Our Voice supports newcomer students in learning English and building community, so every voice gets heard.

How We Started

From Conversation Partners
to Finding Our Voice

It started with a simple idea: what if students helped other students? At Mission High School in San Francisco, a group of us noticed that newcomer students (immigrants, refugees, and undocumented youth) were struggling to find their footing in a new language and a new country.

We partnered with 826 Valencia, San Francisco's beloved literacy nonprofit, to launch an after-school conversation program. What began informally grew into something real: a structured peer mentorship and English learning initiative shaped entirely by students, for students.

In 2024, our program received a $10,000 grant from Youth Funding Youth Ideas. Proof that what we built mattered. Today, Finding Our Voice is the foundation newcomer students at Mission High didn't know they needed.

Gil Silva Yuli Emir Darwing
